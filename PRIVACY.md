# Privacy Policy for SpazRadio

**Effective Date:** November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the SpazRadio Android application (source code available at [github.com/greenjon/spazradio](https://github.com/greenjon/spazradio)) handles user information.

## 1. Information We Collect

SpazRadio is designed to minimize data collection. We do not require account registration, and we do not collect personal identifiers such as your real name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

- **Chat Username & Messages:** If you choose to use the optional chat feature, you will be asked to enter a display username. This username is unverified and can be any alias you choose. Your chosen username and the messages you send are transmitted to the chat server to display them to other active users.
- **Technical & Connection Data:** To stream audio, show the schedule and archives, load visuals, and connect to the chat service, your device communicates directly with our servers at spaz.org and radio.spaz.org. As with standard internet communications, your IP address and basic connection metadata (such as user-agent/device type) are processed by these servers to deliver the audio stream, visuals, and chat messages.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the information strictly to provide and maintain the app's core functionality:

- Delivering the live radio audio stream and archived shows.
- Displaying your chosen username and chat messages to other participants in the public chat room.
- Maintaining server stability and preventing abuse.

## 3. Data Storage and Retention

- **Local Storage:** Your chosen chat username and your app settings (such as stream quality, equalizer, and visuals preferences) are stored locally on your device so you do not have to re-enter them between sessions. You can clear this at any time by clearing the app's data in your Android system settings.
- **Server Logs:** Chat messages and standard connection logs (including IP addresses) are processed temporarily to facilitate real-time communication and server security, and are not used to build user profiles.

## 4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

SpazRadio does not use third-party advertising networks, behavioral tracking SDKs, or analytics trackers. We do not sell, rent, or trade any user data to third parties.

## 5. Children's Privacy

SpazRadio does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Because the optional chat feature allows unmoderated, real-time user interaction, it is not intended for young children.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy and within the project repository at [github.com/greenjon/spazradio](https://github.com/greenjon/spazradio).

## 7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the app's data practices, you can [open an issue on the GitHub repository](https://github.com/greenjon/spazradio/issues).
