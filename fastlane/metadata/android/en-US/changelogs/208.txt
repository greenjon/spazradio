SPAZ Radio 2.0.2: download the show on the radio.
- When the jukebox is playing a show from the archives, a download button sits beside its title. It isn't shown during a live show.
- Downloading over mobile data now asks first and shows the size. Tick "Don't ask again" to skip it, or turn it back on in Settings > Playback.
- Show titles with apostrophes and ampersands display properly.
- Archive rows now switch to the check mark when a download finishes.
