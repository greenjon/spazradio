SPAZ Radio 2.0: Liquid LSD visuals behind the radio.
- The audio-reactive picture from spaz.org/tv replaces the scope. Built in, works offline, follows the music.
- New scenes arrive over the network (Settings > Visuals).
- Controls fade while you watch; tap to bring them back.
- In a pocket or face down, the screen goes dark and the radio plays on.
- Settings for quality, reaction, reduced motion and picture delay.
- Chat: new messages show their time; missed ones appear on reconnect.
